## What Is This?

This platform lets you create a beautiful, personalized webpage where your wedding or event guests can send you gifts through various payment methods. Your page includes:

- **Your event title** (like "Sarah & John's Wedding")
- **A personal message** to your guests
- **Your photos** (main event photo + background images)
- **Multiple gift options** (Bit, PayBox, Venmo, cash apps, etc.)
- **Language support** (English and Hebrew)
- **Dark/Light themes** for comfortable viewing

**Best of all:** Your page is completely private, secure, and only accessible to people you share the link with.

## Quick Start

**Creating your event takes just 5-10 minutes:**

1. **Fill out the form** with your event details (title, message, photos, gift options)
2. **Submit the form** with your email address
3. **Check your email** for your unique event link
4. **Share the link** with your guests via WhatsApp, email, or social media
5. **Done!** Your guests can now send you gifts

## Filling Out the Form

You'll receive a Google Form link from your event platform administrator. Here's what each field means:

### אימייל (Email Address)
**חובה (Required)** - Your email address

- We use this to send you your event link
- If you update your event later, use the **same email** to keep your link
- We never share your email or send you marketing

**Example:** `sarah.john@gmail.com`

### כותרת האירוע (אנגלית) - Event Title (English)
**חובה (Required)** - Your event name in English

- Keep it short and clear
- This appears as the main heading on your page
- Common formats:
  - "Sarah & John's Wedding"
  - "The Smith Family Wedding"
  - "Our Big Day"

**Example:** `Sarah & John's Wedding`

### כותרת האירוע (עברית) - Event Title (Hebrew)
*אופציונלי (Optional)* - Your event name in Hebrew

- Only fill this if you want Hebrew support
- If left blank, English title will show for Hebrew visitors too

**Example:** `החתונה של שרה וג'ון`

### הודעה לאורחים (אנגלית) - Message (English)
**חובה (Required)** - Your message to guests in English

- This appears below the title
- Keep it warm and friendly
- Usually asks guests to send a gift

**Examples:**
- "Send us a gift to help start our new life together!"
- "Your presence is the greatest gift, but if you wish to contribute..."
- "Help us celebrate with a gift!"

### הודעה לאורחים (עברית) - Message (Hebrew)
*אופציונלי (Optional)* - Your message in Hebrew

- Only needed if you want Hebrew support
- If left blank, English message will show for Hebrew visitors

**Example:** `שלחו לנו מתנה כדי לעזור לנו להתחיל את החיים החדשים שלנו!`

### תמונת האירוע (Wedding Image)
*אופציונלי (Optional)* - Your main event photo

- **Recommended size:** 800x600 pixels (landscape)
- **File types:** JPG, PNG
- **Max size:** 10MB (system will optimize it automatically)
- **Tips:**
  - Use a high-quality photo of you two
  - Landscape orientation works best
  - Good lighting and clear faces
  - Will be displayed prominently at top of page

**If you skip this:** A default image will be used

### רקע בהיר (Background Light)
*אופציונלי (Optional)* - Background image for light theme

- **Recommended:** Soft, light-colored landscape photo
- **File types:** JPG, PNG
- **Max size:** 10MB
- **Tips:**
  - Choose a subtle background (not too busy)
  - Light colors work best (whites, creams, pastels)
  - Should not distract from main content

**If you skip this:** A default background will be used

### רקע כהה (Background Dark)
*אופציונלי (Optional)* - Background image for dark theme

- **Recommended:** Darker landscape photo
- **File types:** JPG, PNG
- **Max size:** 10MB
- **Tips:**
  - Choose a darker version or different photo
  - Should be easier on the eyes in dark mode
  - Still not too distracting

**If you skip this:** A default dark background will be used

### Gift Options

The form is organized into sections with page breaks for easier navigation. You can add multiple gift payment methods. Most forms support 3-10 different gift options.

**Note:** The first gift uses simplified field names, while additional gifts are numbered.

#### First Gift Fields:

**שם אמצעי התשלום (אנגלית)** (Payment Method Name - English)
*חובה (Required)* - The payment method name in English

**Examples:**
- "Bit"
- "PayBox"
- "Venmo"
- "Zelle"
- "Cash App"
- "Bank Transfer"

**שם אמצעי התשלום (עברית)** (Payment Method Name - Hebrew)
*אופציונלי (Optional)* - Hebrew name for the payment method

**Examples:**
- "ביט" (Bit)
- "פייבוקס" (PayBox)
- "העברה בנקאית" (Bank Transfer)

**קישורי תשלום** (Payment Links)
*חובה (Required)* - One or more payment links (one per line)

- Add each payment link on a separate line (press Enter after each URL)
- This is where guests will be sent when they click the gift
- **Examples:**
  ```
  https://bit.co.il/app/?pageName=amountSelection&uid=xxx
  https://payboxapp.page.link/xxx
  ```
  
  Or for a single link:
  ```
  https://venmo.com/yourusername
  ```

**Important:** Test your links before submitting! Click them to make sure they work.

**לוגו אמצעי התשלום** (Payment Method Logo)
*אופציונלי (Optional)* - Link to the payment method's logo

- A small image that represents the payment method
- **Examples:**
  - `https://www.bit.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/bit-logo.png`
  - `https://www.payboxapp.com/logo.png`
- **Tips:**
  - Use official company logos
  - Make sure the URL ends in `.png`, `.jpg`, or `.svg`
  - Test the URL in your browser first

**If you skip this:** The gift will show without a logo (text only)

**להוסיף אמצעי תשלום נוסף?** (Add Another Payment Method?)
*חובה (Required)* - Choose whether to add more payment options

- Select "כן / Yes" to add another payment method (continues to next section)
- Select "לא - סיימתי / No - I'm done" to finish and submit the form

**Note:** The form uses smart navigation - choosing "No" will automatically submit your form without showing additional gift sections.

---

#### Additional Gift Fields (מתנה 2, מתנה 3, etc.):

For gifts 2 and beyond, the field names include numbers:

- **מתנה X - שם (אנגלית)** - Payment method name in English (optional)
- **מתנה X - שם (עברית)** - Payment method name in Hebrew (optional)
- **מתנה X - קישורים** - Payment links, one per line (optional)
- **מתנה X - לוגו** - Logo URL (optional)
- **להוסיף אמצעי תשלום נוסף?** - Add another? (required if not last gift)

### Recommended Number of Gifts

- **Minimum:** 1 gift option
- **Recommended:** 2-3 gift options (gives guests choices)
- **Maximum:** Usually 5-10 (depends on form configuration)

**Pro tip:** Include both Israeli payment methods (Bit, PayBox) and international options (PayPal, Venmo) if you have guests from different countries.

## After Submitting

### What Happens Next?

1. **Instant processing** - The system receives your form
2. **Creating your page** - Your event page is generated and encrypted
3. **Email sent** - You receive an email with your unique link (usually within 1-2 minutes)
4. **Ready to share** - Your page is live and ready for guests!

### Your Email

You'll receive an email that looks like this:

```
Subject: Your Event URL is Ready!

Hi [Your Name],

Your event page has been created successfully!

Your unique event URL:
https://[platform].github.io/wedding_gifts/?event=abc123-uuid#secretkey456

IMPORTANT:
- Share this FULL link with your guests (including everything after the #)
- Keep this link private - anyone with the link can see your event
- To update your event, fill out the form again with the same email address

Your event page will be available in 1-2 minutes.

Congratulations and best wishes!
```

**Important notes:**
- The link is **long** - you must copy the entire thing including the `#` and everything after it
- This is your **only** link - save it somewhere safe
- The `#` part is your encryption key - without it, the page won't load

### First Time Viewing

1. **Copy the full URL** from your email (triple-click to select all)
2. **Paste it** in your browser
3. **Wait 1-2 minutes** if you see an error (the system needs time to prepare)
4. **Refresh** your browser (F5 or refresh button)
5. **Your page loads** with your custom content!

## Sharing Your Event

### How to Share

**WhatsApp:**
1. Open WhatsApp Web or app
2. Paste the full URL in a message
3. WhatsApp will show a preview (if available)
4. Send to individual guests or groups

**Email:**
1. Compose an email to your guests
2. Write a short message
3. Paste the full URL
4. Send

**Facebook/Instagram:**
1. Create a post or story
2. Paste the link
3. Add a personal message
4. Share (consider privacy settings!)

**Text Message:**
1. Copy the full URL
2. Send via SMS to guests
3. Note: Long URLs might split into multiple texts

### Shortening Your Link (Optional)

The event URL is long. You can shorten it using a service like:
- **Bitly:** [bitly.com](https://bitly.com)
- **TinyURL:** [tinyurl.com](https://tinyurl.com)

**Steps:**
1. Go to the shortening service
2. Paste your full event URL (including `#` and key)
3. Create a short link
4. Share the short link instead

**Important:** Make sure the shortening service preserves the `#` part!

## Updating Your Event

Need to change something? No problem!

### How to Update

1. **Find the original form** (same link you used before)
2. **Fill it out again** with:
   - **Same email address** (this is critical!)
   - Updated information (new title, different gifts, new photos, etc.)
3. **Submit the form**
4. **Check your email** - you'll receive the **same link** as before

### What Gets Updated

- **Same URL** - Your old link keeps working (don't need to reshare)
- **New content** - Page shows your updated information
- **New photos** - If you upload new images, they replace old ones

### How Long Until Updates Show?

- **Immediate** - Changes are saved right away
- **1-2 minutes** - CDN cache updates globally
- **Guests need to refresh** - They might need to refresh their browser to see changes

### Update Tips

- **Test before sharing** - Update once to get everything right
- **Notify guests** - If you change gift links, tell guests to refresh
- **Don't change too often** - Guests might have bookmarked the old version

## Privacy & Security

### Is My Event Private?

**Yes!** Your event is encrypted and private:

- **Encrypted storage** - Your event details are encrypted before being saved
- **Unique link** - Only people with your full link can access your page
- **No public listing** - Your event doesn't appear in any directory or search
- **Secure key** - The `#` part of your URL is your encryption key, never sent to servers

### Who Can See My Event?

**Only people you share the link with:**
- Anyone with the full URL (including `#` and key) can view your page
- Without the full URL, it's impossible to find your event
- No one can "browse" or discover your event randomly

### What About My Email?

- **Stored encrypted** - Your email is encrypted in the system
- **Not shared** - Never shared with third parties
- **No marketing** - You won't receive marketing emails
- **Only purpose** - Used only to send you your event link and allow updates

### What About My Photos?

- **Stored encrypted** - Photos are encrypted before storage
- **Optimized automatically** - Large photos are compressed (faster loading)
- **Only accessible** - Via your event link
- **Your content** - You own your photos, platform just hosts them

### Best Practices

1. **Share privately** - Send links via direct message, not public posts
2. **Trust your guests** - Only share with people you know
3. **Monitor sharing** - If someone posts publicly, ask them to remove it
4. **No sensitive info** - Don't put sensitive details in your message
5. **Save your link** - Keep a copy in a secure note or password manager

### What If Someone Shares My Link Publicly?

- Anyone with the link can view your page
- You can't "take it down" - the link always works
- Best option: Create a new event with a new email (new link)
- Prevention: Educate guests to keep links private

**No!** Just fill out a form - the system does everything else automatically.

### How much does it cost?

**Free!** The platform uses free services (Google Forms, GitHub, CDN). No charges, no subscriptions.

**Absolutely!** Works for:
- Weddings
- Birthdays
- Anniversaries
- Baby showers

- Any event where you want to collect gifts

**Indefinitely**, as long as the platform administrator keeps the system running. Your page doesn't expire.

Contact your platform administrator. They can remove your event from the system if needed.

**Yes!** Use **different email addresses** for each event. Each gets its own unique link.

**No!** Guests just click your link and choose a gift option. No signup, no login.

### What languages are supported?

Not directly through the form. The platform uses a standard, elegant design. For custom designs, you'd need to modify the code (technical).

See [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting) below.

### Can I use my own domain name?

Not through the standard setup. Advanced users can configure custom domains with GitHub Pages.

### Is this mobile-friendly?

**Yes!** Your event page works beautifully on:
- Phones (iOS, Android)
- Tablets
- Laptops
- Desktop computers

### What payment methods can I use?

Any payment method with a web link:
- **Israeli:** Bit, PayBox, Pepper Pay

## Troubleshooting

### I didn't receive the email

**Wait 5 minutes** - Sometimes emails are delayed

**Check spam/junk folder** - The email might be filtered

**Verify email address** - Make sure you entered it correctly in the form

**Re-submit form** - Fill out the form again with your email

**Contact administrator** - If still no email after 10 minutes

### The link shows "Event Not Found"

**Wait 1-2 minutes** - System needs time to prepare your event

**Check the full URL** - Make sure you copied everything including `#` and the key

**Refresh browser** - Press F5 or Ctrl+R (Cmd+R on Mac)

**Clear cache** - Try clearing browser cache or use incognito/private mode

**Try different browser** - Test in Chrome, Firefox, or Safari

### My photos aren't showing

**Wait a few minutes** - Large photos take time to process

**Check file size** - Files over 10MB might fail (resize before uploading)

**Verify file type** - Use JPG or PNG only

**Re-submit form** - Try uploading images again

**Use smaller files** - Compress images before upload (use tinypng.com)

### Gift buttons don't work

**Check URLs** - Make sure gift URLs are correct and complete

**Test links** - Click them yourself to verify they work

**Update event** - Re-submit form with corrected URLs

### Page loads but looks wrong

**Hard refresh** - Ctrl+Shift+R (or Cmd+Shift+R on Mac)

**Clear cache** - Clear browser cache completely

**Try different browser** - Test in another browser

**Check on mobile** - Test on your phone

### Link is too long to share

**Use a URL shortener:**
- [bitly.com](https://bitly.com)
- [tinyurl.com](https://tinyurl.com)

**Make sure to:**
- Copy the FULL original URL
- Keep the `#` and key intact
- Test the shortened link before sharing

### I want to change my email

**You can't change the email** associated with an existing event.

**Options:**
1. Keep using the current email for updates
2. Create a new event with a new email (you'll get a new link)

### Someone else sees my event data

**Check what you shared:**
- Did you share the full URL (including `#` and key)?
- That's normal - anyone with the full URL can view

**If you didn't share:**
- It's very unlikely someone found your link randomly
- Check if a guest shared it further

**Want to make it private again:**
- Create a new event with a new email
- New link = fresh privacy

## Need Help?

### Contact Your Administrator

Your event platform administrator can help with:
- Technical issues
- Deleted events
- Form access
- System problems

### Common Issues Are Usually:

- Copying incomplete URL (missing `#` and key)
- Not waiting 1-2 minutes for system to process
- Browser cache showing old version
- Spam filter catching the email

### Before Contacting Support:

1. Try troubleshooting steps above
2. Wait 5 minutes (many issues resolve automatically)
3. Clear browser cache and try again
4. Test in a different browser or device

### When Asking for Help, Include:

- What you're trying to do
- What error message you see (if any)
- When you submitted the form
- Your email address (so they can look up your event)
- Browser and device you're using
